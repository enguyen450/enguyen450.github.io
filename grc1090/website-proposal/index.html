Introduction

To Bee Or Not To Bee

We are a beekeeping company that ethically harvests honey and sells honey products. Form jars of honey, to honey combs, to even live bees, we sell everything bee related.

Target audience

Our target audience are people who are looking to buy honey products or are looking into raising their own bees. We also offer resources for those that are just starting their own beekeeping hobby!

The primary task and goal of this business is to sell honey and other bee related items. To spread the message of bees and their impact on the world and how to correctly take of them.
